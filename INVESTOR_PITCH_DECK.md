## Slide 3 — The Insight: Voice Creates Accountability

### Speaker Script
> "Here is what we discovered: **Voice creates commitment**. When you say something out loud to another entity — even an AI — you are dramatically more likely to follow through. It is the difference between typing 'go to the gym' into a text box and having someone call you and ask, 'Are you going to the gym today?'
>
> The human brain is wired for voice. We evolved with conversation, not with notifications. A phone call demands a response. It is interruptsive by design. And that interruptive quality — which apps try to avoid — is exactly what procrastinators need.
>
> dialer.best turns the phone from a distraction device into an **accountability device**."

## Slide 7 — Business Model

### Speaker Script
> "We operate a **prepaid credit model** — users buy credits and spend them on call minutes. Think of it as a 'pay-as-you-go' productivity coach.
>
> — **Rate:** $1.00 per minute of AI phone conversation
> — **Average session:** 12 minutes of calls per day (~$12/day, ~$360/month)
> — **Minimum refill:** $5 via Stripe
> — **Preset refill amounts:** $5, $10, $25, $50
>
> The economics are compelling. Our primary cost is the Vapi AI voice API (~$0.12/min) and LLM inference (~$0.03/min). That gives us a **gross margin of approximately 85%** at scale.
>
> We have also designed a three-tier future pricing model:
> — **Free Tier:** 1 morning call + basic scheduling (acquisition engine)
> — **Pro Tier:** Unlimited calls + priority follow-ups + PDF reports ($29/mo)
> — **Business Tier:** Team accounts, admin dashboard, analytics ($99/mo)
>
> Our LTV-to-CAC ratio at current organic acquisition is already above **15:1**."

## Slide 8 — Competitive Moat

### Speaker Script
> "Our moat is three layers deep:
>
> **1. Technology moat.** We have built a sophisticated AI orchestration layer on top of Vapi that dynamically builds ephemeral AI agents per call. Our task extraction engine parses natural conversation into structured todo items with priorities, deadlines, and follow-up schedules — all in real time. We use OpenRouter (DeepSeek + Gemini) for LLM inference and Gemini for audio transcription and vision.
>
> **2. Data moat.** Every call generates a transcript. Every transcript trains a better model. Our system learns your speech patterns, your productivity habits, your procrastination triggers. After 30 days with dialer.best, the AI knows you better than any task app ever could.
>
> **3. Switching cost moat.** You cannot switch away easily because the AI is literally in your daily routine. It calls you. It builds a relationship. Users develop an emotional attachment to 'their' AI voice — we offer 6 premium ElevenLabs voices. That is not a feature you can copy in a weekend."
