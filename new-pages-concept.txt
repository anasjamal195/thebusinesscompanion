PAGE 1: GOALCHASER FOR CORPORATE IT
Page purpose

The Corporate IT page should explain how GoalChaser works inside a modern IT organization.

The core problem:

Teams already have Jira, Trello, Asana, ClickUp, Notion, GitHub, Slack, Teams, etc.

The problem isn't necessarily the lack of tools.

The problem is:

People still have to update them, communicate progress, report blockers, attend meetings, and managers still have to chase status.

GoalChaser becomes the active productivity layer around those existing tools.

The original material already communicates this concept: GoalChaser connects to existing tools, reads ongoing tasks, follows up through calls, and generates employee progress reports.
